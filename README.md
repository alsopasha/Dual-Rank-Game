# Dual-Rank

A 5×5 slide-and-merge game. Tiles come in two families, 2 and 3. Matching tiles of the same family merge and climb toward ^10. Win by reaching ^10 on both families.

Play with arrows, WASD, or swipe. **Engine** lets the solver play itself. **New** starts over.

Built out of personal curiosity, then written up as an academic paper. The paper will be published later.
